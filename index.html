Butuan Heritage Map

Archaeological, historic, and museum sites of Butuan City. Filter them, find the nearest one, and see how they cluster along the Agusan River.

    Spatial analysis

    These tools work on the sites that match your filters.

    Nearest heritage site

    Use your location or click any point on the map. Distances are straight-line.

    Walking zones

    Draws a circle around every site and merges the overlaps, so you can see which sites sit within walking distance of each other.

    Sites per barangay

    Shades each barangay by how many sites it contains.

    Distance to the Agusan River

    Measures how far each site is from the river that made Butuan a trading port.
